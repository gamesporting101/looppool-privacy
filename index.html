Loop Pool Anomaly - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Who we are

This policy explains what data the mobile game Loop Pool Anomaly collects, why, and the choices you have. Loop Pool is published by Creaper Reaper Games, contact creaperreapergames@gmail.com. It applies to the Android game with the package name com.creeperreaper.looppool, and to any site or store page that links to this policy.

Loop Pool Anomaly has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

What we collect

If you tap Allow on the first-launch screen, the game collects the data below. If you tap No thanks, none of it is collected through analytics, and ads are set to non-personalised (see Your choices).

Gameplay events (Google Firebase Analytics). Anonymous events about how a run goes: when a run or round starts, which anomaly it uses, when you find it, when you finish or lose a round and why (time up, out of air, caught), how many wrong taps and lives you had, how many seconds a round took, when a game ends, and whether an ad was shown or failed to load.

Device and app data (collected automatically by Firebase). Device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate country or region derived from your IP address, session length, and a random app-instance identifier. On Android, Firebase may also use the device advertising ID.

Ad data (Start.io). To show ads, Start.io's SDK receives your advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be estimated), device and app details, information about the ads you see and tap, and, where the advertising ID is available, information about other apps installed on your device. It may also contact Start.io in the background, for example after your device restarts, to update its settings. The game does not ask for location permission, so no precise location is shared. With personalised ads off, Start.io still receives the technical data needed to show and count ads, but is told not to use it for personalised advertising.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

We use gameplay data to find out where players get stuck, which anomalies are too easy or too hard, and whether the game crashes or runs badly on certain devices, so we can fix and balance it. We use ad data to show ads that fund the game.

Where data protection law such as the EU/UK GDPR applies, our legal basis for analytics and for personalised advertising is your consent, which you give on the first-launch screen. You can withdraw it at any time (see Your choices); withdrawing does not affect anything collected before that.

Who receives the data

We do not sell your data. It is processed on our behalf, or independently, by these providers:

  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics (Google LLC and Google Ireland Ltd): game analytics. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Start.io (formerly StartApp): the only ad network in the game. It shows the rewarded and interstitial ads and works with advertisers and ad partners, who may receive this data to deliver and measure ads. Privacy policy: https://www.start.io/policy/. US residents can also opt out of the sale or sharing of their data at start.io/do-not-sell-or-sharemy-personal-information.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

Your choices

First launch. The game asks once whether you allow anonymous play statistics (Google Firebase) and personalised ads. You can tap Allow or No thanks, and you can play the full game either way.

Change or withdraw consent. Tap the Privacy button on the title screen at any time to switch analytics and personalised ads on or off. Your choice applies from then on.

If you decline. The game does not start Firebase Analytics, and ads are requested as non-personalised. Ads may still appear, since they fund the game.

Advertising ID. You can reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).

Uninstalling the game removes the choice stored on your device.

Retention, security, transfers and children

Retention. Firebase keeps event-level analytics data for a limited period set in our Firebase project (we use the shortest practical setting, up to 14 months). Start.io keeps data under its own policy.

Security. Data is sent over encrypted connections. We do not run our own server for player data, and the game stores only your consent choice on your device.

International transfers. Google, Start.io and their partners may process data in countries outside your own, using safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where the law requires.

Children. Loop Pool Anomaly is a horror game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights and how to contact us

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. California residents can also opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising; you can do this by choosing No thanks or switching consent off under Privacy.

Because the game has no accounts, we usually cannot identify you from our side. To use a right that needs identification, email us at creaperreapergames@gmail.com and include your advertising ID if you want us to look for it (Android Settings > Google > Ads). You can also exercise many rights directly with Google and Start.io through their privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects or which providers we use, we will update this page and the effective date below, and the game will ask for your consent again if the change requires it.
